Quick Info

Leah

Aspiring artist living just outside of Pelican Town.

Location: Cindersap Forest
Relationship Status: Single
Birthday: Winter 23

Relationships

Leah lives alone on the outskirts of Pelican Town. She moved from the city to Stardew Valley to pursue her dream of being an artist. She is friends with Elliot, a writer who lives on the beach.

Loved Gifts

Leah's loved gifts include goat cheese, poppyseed muffin, salad, stir fry, truffle, vegetable medley, and wine. She also loves all of the Universal Loves: golden pumpkin, magic rock candy, pearl, prismatic shard, rabbit's foot, and stardrop tea.
